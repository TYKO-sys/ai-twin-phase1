Your AI Twin

Set up in 5 minutes. No technical skills needed.

1

Install the App

First, you need to install one app on your phone. It's free and open source.

Download the App

This opens F-Droid (a safe app store). Tap "Download APK" then install.

2

Open It and Paste This

Open the app you just installed. You'll see a black screen. Copy and paste this exact text, then press Enter:

bash <(curl -s https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TYKO-sys/ai-twin-phase1/main/install.sh)
Press and hold on the black screen to paste. Then press Enter.
3

Follow the Setup Wizard

A setup page will open automatically in your browser. It will guide you through getting your AI keys.

The wizard takes about 3 minutes. You'll need:
• A Telegram account
• A Google account (for free AI access)
That's it.
4

Allow Auto-Start (Optional)

To make sure your twin is always there when you restart your phone, install this helper app:

Get the Auto-Start Helper
After installing, just open it once and close it. That's all you need to do.

Also go to your phone's Settings → Apps → find the apps you just installed → Battery → set to "Unrestricted"

✓

You're Done!

Your AI twin is now running. Open Telegram and find your bot. Send it a message to say hi.

Your twin will check in with you every morning at 9am and every evening at 9pm. It remembers everything you tell it.
