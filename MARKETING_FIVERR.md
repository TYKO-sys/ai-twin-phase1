### FAQ

**Q: Does this work on iPhone?**
A: Not yet. The backend runs on Android. iPhone support is coming in a future version.

**Q: Will it cost me money every month?**
A: No. The free AI tiers cover normal use. If you want faster responses and zero rate limits, you can add $5 to an AI provider — that lasts months. But $0/month works fine for most people.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Your conversations are stored locally on your phone as plain text files. Your messages go to AI providers (Google, OpenRouter, etc.) for processing — that's how the twin thinks. The bot is locked to your Telegram account. No one else can talk to it.

**Q: What if something breaks?**
A: Send the twin a `/fix` command. It clears errors and reconnects automatically. If that doesn't work, I'm available for support.

**Q: Can I customize the personality?**
A: Yes. The Standard and Premium tiers include custom personality tuning. You can also edit it yourself — I'll show you how.

**Q: What if I don't like it?**
A: You pay when you're satisfied. If the installation doesn't work on your phone, you don't pay.
